BMW 635 project

2025Project

My project car: a BMW 635, from the E24 6 Series coupes BMW built from 1976 to 1989. Mine is flat gray on silver cross-spoke wheels. This page is the running log, newest at the top.

Why this car

The E24 is one of the best-looking cars BMW ever made. Long hood, shark nose, thin pillars, and a greenhouse you can actually see out of. It's old enough to be simple to work on and good enough to be worth the effort.
