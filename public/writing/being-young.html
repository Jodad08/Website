Why being young shows that anything is possible

2024Essay

People talk about being young like it's a disadvantage in business. No money, no experience, no network. I think it's the opposite. Being young is the cheapest time you'll ever have to try something hard.

Failing costs almost nothing

If I start something and it doesn't work, I don't lose a mortgage or a family's income. I lose some time and learn a lot. The worst case is a good story and a set of skills I didn't have before. That math only gets worse as you get older, so the time to take the swing is now.

You don't know what's "impossible" yet

Experience teaches you what usually doesn't work. That's useful, but it also stops people from trying things that might. When you're young you haven't learned all the reasons something can't be done, so you just go do it. Sometimes you're wrong. Sometimes everyone else was.

People want to help

Send a cold email as a student and people answer it. Ask a dumb question and people explain. Nobody expects you to have it figured out, which means you get to learn in public without much judgment. That window closes, so use it.

So I'm using it

I started by selling websites to small businesses through Marketing Meteor, mostly to learn how a business actually runs. Now I'm building Hermie. Neither of those required me to be older. They just required me to start.
