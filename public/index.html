Hermie

Working onsite at dental practices, I watched front desks lose hours on insurance hold lines. So I built Hermie: it verifies every scheduled patient overnight and writes the full benefits breakdown into the chart before anyone walks in.

How
Clearinghouse check first, payer website second, an AI voice agent calls the payer last. Each step only fills what the one before it missed.
Where
Writes straight into Dentrix, Eaglesoft, and Open Dental through a bridge that runs inside the office.
Now
A first pilot practice runs it every week with zero staff involvement. I founded it and built it.

How I work

Start where the work happens

Hermie came from watching dental front desks on hold with insurers. Quintet started with an interview with a locksmith about how he pays bills. I look for the problem in person before I build anything.

Scope it, then ship it

At Marketing Meteor I take each client from first call to a live, hosted site. At hackathons I've scoped products down to what a small team can finish in a weekend, and placed with one.

Lead when it's live

As Sound Crew Head in high school I directed a crew of technicians through live shows where nothing could go down. It taught me to plan for failure and stay calm when something breaks.

Keep systems running

I keep servers, networks and backups running for dental practices under HIPAA rules, and run a five-node Kubernetes cluster at home. I understand the systems I'm asking a team to build.
