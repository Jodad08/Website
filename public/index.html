Hermie

Dental offices lose hours every morning on insurance hold music. Hermie verifies every scheduled patient overnight and writes the full benefits breakdown into the chart before anyone walks in.

How
Clearinghouse check first, payer website second, an AI voice agent calls the payer last. Each step only fills what the one before it missed.
Where
Writes straight into Dentrix, Eaglesoft, and Open Dental through a bridge that runs inside the office.
